帮我润色一下这段引言，准备投 Soil Biology & Biochemistry：

Nitrogen is an essential nutrient for plant growth. Soil microorganisms play an important role in nitrogen cycling. Smith et al. (2015) found that drought reduced microbial biomass in grassland soils. Wang et al. (2018) found that rewetting after drought caused a pulse of CO2 release. Li et al. (2020) reported that nitrogen mineralization increased after rewetting. However, little is known about the effects of drought on soil nitrogen cycling. In this study, we conducted an investigation of the effects of drought-rewetting cycles on soil nitrogen mineralization, microbial biomass and enzyme activities in a semi-arid grassland. Our objectives were (1) to compare two methods for measuring microbial biomass, and (2) to examine the response of N mineralization to drought-rewetting cycles.
